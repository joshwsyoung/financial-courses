**Step 4 tests** you can apply to each option:

- **Rules:** does it break any law, regulation or firm policy?
- **Interests:** whose interests does it put first? Is the client harmed?
- **Headline:** would you be comfortable seeing it reported in the press?
- **Explain:** could you justify it openly to your manager, the client and the regulator?
