The Standards fall into seven groups. The first three face outwards, to the profession, the market and clients. The rest cover your employer, your work, your conflicts and your link to CFA Institute.

Each Standard groups a few specific duties. You do not need the official wording, but you do need to know what each one is about.

- **I Professionalism:** know and follow the law, stay independent and objective, do not misrepresent, avoid misconduct. Keeping your competence up to date belongs here too.
- **II Integrity of capital markets:** do not trade on or pass on material non-public information, and do not manipulate prices or volumes.
- **III Duties to clients:** loyalty, prudence and care; fair dealing; suitability; fair performance figures; confidentiality.
- **IV Duties to employers:** loyalty to your employer, consent for competing outside pay, proper supervision.
- **V Investment analysis, recommendations and actions:** a reasonable basis, clear communication, good records.
- **VI Conflicts of interest:** disclose conflicts, put client trades first, disclose referral fees.
- **VII Responsibilities as a member or candidate:** protect the integrity of CFA Institute exams and describe the designation accurately.
