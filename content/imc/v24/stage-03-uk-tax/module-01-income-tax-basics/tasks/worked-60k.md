**Question.** Sam earns a salary of **£60,000** in 2026-27. Sam has no other income and lives in England. How much income tax does Sam pay?

**Step 1: take off the personal allowance.**
£60,000 − £12,570 = **£47,430** taxable income.

**Step 2: fill the basic rate band first.**
The basic rate band is £37,700.
£37,700 × 20% = **£7,540**.

**Step 3: tax the rest at the higher rate.**
£47,430 − £37,700 = £9,730.
£9,730 × 40% = **£3,892**.

**Step 4: add the slices.**
£7,540 + £3,892 = **£11,432**.

**Check.** £11,432 ÷ £60,000 is an average rate of about 19%. That is well below 40%, as expected, because only the top slice pays the higher rate.

**Common mistake:** taxing all £47,430 at 40% (giving £18,972). Bands are slices, not a single rate.
