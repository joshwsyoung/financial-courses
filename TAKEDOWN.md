# Takedown process

We only publish original content. If you believe something here infringes your rights:

1. Open an issue using the **Takedown request** template, or email the maintainer listed on the GitHub profile if the details are sensitive.
2. Tell us which file or page, what you believe it copies, and your contact details.
3. A maintainer will acknowledge within **2 working days** and, where the complaint is plausible, hide the content from the live site straight away while we look into it.
4. We will either remove or rewrite the content, or explain why we believe it is original, within **10 working days**.

We keep a short record of each request and outcome in the closed issue.
